## 2. DIDASCALIA
When the line is exactly 3, the game ends on exactly 3 about one time in six. When the line is 3.5, it cannot happen. Not once. Not ever.

Half a point. Two completely different numbers.

Our weekly report counts this for every line on the board, instead of telling you a game "feels like a trap."

## 3. TRADUZIONE
Sulla linea 3 la partita puo' finire esattamente li', circa una volta su sei. Sulla 3.5 non puo' succedere mai. Mezzo punto, due numeri completamente diversi.
