## 2. DIDASCALIA
A 10-point margin is a touchdown plus a field goal: the most ordinary scoring sequence in the sport. A 9 needs three field goals, or a touchdown with a missed extra point plus a field goal.

Nothing about football produces a 9 naturally, and the count shows it. The 9 is rarer than the 11 sitting two points above it.

Knowing which numbers are crowded and which are nearly empty is most of the job.

## 3. TRADUZIONE
Il 10 e' touchdown piu' field goal, la sequenza piu' normale che esista. Per fare 9 serve un percorso strano. Risultato: il 9 e' piu' raro perfino dell'11.
