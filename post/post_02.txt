## 2. DIDASCALIA
Before Week 3 we wrote a rule down and published it: when the forecast says 11 mph of wind or more and the roof is open, expect fewer points.

Three games qualified. All three went over.

The rule goes up before the games, the count goes up after, whichever way it went. Three games prove nothing - that is exactly why the count keeps running all season and we don't get to touch it.

Full week reports, free, no sign-up. Link in bio.

## 3. TRADUZIONE
Regola scritta e pubblicata prima della week 3: vento previsto da 11 mph in su e tetto aperto, aspettati meno punti. Tre partite rientravano, tutte e tre finite sopra. La regola prima, il conteggio dopo, comunque vada.
