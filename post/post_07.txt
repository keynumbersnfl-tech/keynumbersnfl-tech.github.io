## 2. DIDASCALIA
Home field is still worth something. It is worth noticeably less than it used to be, and almost all of the drop is recent.

The small dots are single seasons. We show them on purpose: this number bounces a lot year to year, so any one season tells you close to nothing. The shift between the eras is the part that holds up.

If you learned this sport ten years ago, one of the things you learned is weaker than you think it is.
